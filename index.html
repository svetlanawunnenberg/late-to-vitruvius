The Scriptorium

Definitions are kept short on purpose — this is a drill, not a dictionary. They are shown in whichever wording you picked in the yard — Plain or Exact. Terms marked VITRUVIUS appear in De architectura itself; the rest are later codification, chiefly Renaissance.

On any machine you own

How to play

It is a web page. There is nothing to install, nothing to sign into, and it works offline once loaded.

The game itself

  1. Pick a chariot. Each is a different race, not a different colour.
  2. Pomegranates cross the road. Click or tap one and it splits open, scattering its seeds — that buys you time on the water clock.
  3. A gilded pomegranate carries a scroll. Open it and the scroll unrolls with a term.
  4. Answer it. Correct answers move you along the road; the road is measured in modules, eight to a leg.
  5. Three legs to the lecture. Arrive before the clock empties, and arrive having learned something.

Sound

  • Off by default. The switch is in the top corner of every screen, and it remembers.
  • The hooves quicken when the run is going well and hold while you read a scroll. There is no audio file — it is all made in the browser, so it costs nothing to load.

Plain or Exact

  • Set in the yard, before you start. Plain describes each term the way you would point at it on a building; Exact puts it the way a treatise does.
  • It changes nothing else. The same eighty terms, the same eight scrolls a leg, the same clock. Only the sentences around the term are different — on Exact they lean on the other terms of art, so you need the whole vocabulary to read the clue.

Typing answers

  • Spelling is forgiving. Case, apostrophes, hyphens and accents are all ignored — egg and dart and Egg-and-Dart both pass.
  • One or two letters out and it says so, and gives the letter back to you to try again.
  • Enter submits.

Mac · Safari or Chrome

  • Open the link. Click the fruit with the trackpad or mouse.
  • To keep it: Cmd D to bookmark.

Windows · Edge or Chrome

  • Open the link. Click the fruit.
  • To keep it: Ctrl D to bookmark.

Sharing your run

  • At the door, Share this run hands your three scores to the phone's own share sheet — Messages, Instagram, wherever.
  • On a laptop the same button copies the run to the clipboard instead, ready to paste.

iPhone & iPad

  • Open the link in Safari, then tap the pomegranates.
  • To make it behave like an app: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with no browser bars.
  • On a phone, Tap the meaning is the better answer mode — typing Latin against a clock on a small keyboard is no fun.
  • An iPad with a keyboard is the best of both: use Type the term.
